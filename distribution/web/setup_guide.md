# Quick 3-Step Setup Guide: Duchesne School Assistant (Web Chat)

Set up your personal Duchesne AI School Assistant in under 60 seconds on **ChatGPT**, **Claude**, or **Google Gemini**!

---

## ⚡ 3-Step Quickstart

### Step 1: Copy the Universal Prompt
Open and copy the entire text from [`universal_parent_prompt.md`](universal_parent_prompt.md).

### Step 2: Paste into Your Favorite AI Web Chat

Choose your preferred platform:

#### 🤖 Option A: Anthropic Claude ([claude.ai](https://claude.ai))
1. Navigate to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and sign in.
2. **For Claude Projects (Recommended):**
   - Click **Projects** > **New Project** and name it **"Duchesne School Assistant"**.
   - Under **Project Instructions**, paste the prompt text and click **Save**.
   - *(Optional)* Upload `duchesne_knowledge_base.md` to Project Knowledge.
3. **For Regular Chat:**
   - Start a new chat, paste the prompt as your first message, and press Enter.

#### 💬 Option B: OpenAI ChatGPT ([chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com))
1. Navigate to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) and sign in.
2. **For Custom GPTs (Plus / Team / Pro):**
   - Click **Explore GPTs** > **Create**.
   - Under the **Configure** tab, set Name to **"Duchesne School Assistant"**.
   - In **Instructions**, paste the prompt text.
   - Click **Save / Create**.
3. **For Custom Instructions (Free & All Users):**
   - Click your profile icon > **Customize ChatGPT**.
   - Under *"How would you like ChatGPT to respond?"*, paste the prompt text and save.
4. **For Regular Chat:**
   - Start a new conversation and paste the prompt in your opening message.

#### ✨ Option C: Google Gemini ([gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com))
1. Navigate to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) and sign in.
2. **For Gemini Gems (Advanced / Workspace):**
   - Click **Gem Manager** (in the left sidebar) > **New Gem**.
   - Name your Gem **"Duchesne School Assistant"**.
   - In the **Instructions** box, paste the prompt text and click **Save**.
3. **For Regular Chat:**
   - Start a new chat, paste the prompt as your initial message, and send.

---

### Step 3: Tell Your Assistant About Your Family

Once pasted, the assistant will greet you:
> *"Welcome to the Duchesne School Assistant! ❤️ Charger Pride! To personalize announcements, events, and schedules for your family, which division(s) and grade(s) are your daughter(s) in?..."*

Reply with your daughter's division or grade:
- *"My daughter is in PK4 in Lower School."*
- *"I have a 7th grader in Middle School and a 10th grader in Upper School."*

Now you're ready! You can:
- **Paste school emails or Friday letters:** Ask *"Can you summarize this Friday letter and list action items?"*
- **Ask about dress code:** *"Is tomorrow a formal dress day or regular uniform for Lower School?"*
- **Check lunch menus:** *"What's on the Lower School lunch menu for PK4 today?"*
- **Find spirit gear:** *"Where can I buy a Duchesne hoodie or plaid hair bows?"*
- **Get portal links:** *"Give me the link to Veracross messages and communication preferences."*

---

## 🔒 Privacy Reminder
Remember: **NEVER share your Veracross, Toddle, or Magnus passwords with any AI assistant.** Your assistant never needs your password. Simply paste text from notices or emails directly into the chat!
