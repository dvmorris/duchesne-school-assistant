# Duchesne Academy of the Sacred Heart — Canonical Knowledge Base

Universal canonical reference for parents, students, faculty, and AI assistants supporting **Duchesne Academy of the Sacred Heart** in Houston, Texas.

---

## 1. Campus & General Information

* **Institution:** Duchesne Academy of the Sacred Heart
* **Address:** 10202 Memorial Dr, Houston, TX 77024
* **Main Telephone:** (713) 468-8211
* **Website:** [https://www.duchesne.org](https://www.duchesne.org)
* **Founded:** 1960 (Member of the Network of Sacred Heart Schools)
* **School Colors:** Red, White, and Navy Blue
* **Mascot:** The Chargers

### Sacred Heart Goals
All education and campus life at Duchesne are rooted in the **Five Goals of Sacred Heart Education**:
1. **Goal I:** A personal and active faith in God.
2. **Goal II:** A deep respect for intellectual values.
3. **Goal III:** A social awareness which impels to action.
4. **Goal IV:** The building of community as a Christian value.
5. **Goal V:** Personal growth in an atmosphere of wise freedom.

## 2. Academic Divisions & Bell Schedules

Duchesne Academy serves young women from Pre-Kindergarten 3 through 12th grade across three distinct divisions:

### Lower School (PK3–Grade 4)
* **Grades:** Pre-K 3, Pre-K 4, Kindergarten, Grades 1–4
* **School Hours:** 7:55 AM – 3:15 PM
  * *PK3 / PK4 Dismissal:* Half-day dismissal at 12:00 PM or full-day dismissal at 3:15 PM
* **Daily Lunch Times:**
  * **PK3 / PK4 Lunch:** **10:55 AM** – 11:25 AM Central
  * **Kindergarten – 2nd Grade:** 11:30 AM – 12:00 PM
  * **3rd Grade – 4th Grade:** 12:05 PM – 12:35 PM
* **Learning Management System:** Toddle LMS (`https://web.toddleapp.com`) for early childhood / lower school homerooms and portfolio updates.

### Middle School (Grades 5–8)
* **Grades:** 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th
* **School Hours:** 7:55 AM – 3:30 PM
* **Structure:** Departmentalized schedule, daily advisory, competitive middle school athletics (SPC Junior division), fine arts rotations, and class retreats.

### Upper School (Grades 9–12)
* **Grades:** 9th (Freshmen), 10th (Sophomores), 11th (Juniors), 12th (Seniors)
* **School Hours:** 8:00 AM – 3:30 PM
* **Structure:** College preparatory curriculum, AP and honors courses, varsity athletics (Southwest Preparatory Conference - SPC), college counseling, campus ministry, and Senior Capstone.

## 3. Uniform Regulations & Dress Code

Duchesne Academy upholds standard uniform policies through **Mills Uniform Company** ([https://www.millswear.com](https://www.millswear.com)).

### A. Formal / Liturgical Dress Days
Required for All-School Masses, special liturgies, First Friday services, and formal ceremonies:
* **Lower School:** White Peter Pan collar blouse, school plaid jumper (PK3–2nd) or plaid skirt (3rd–4th), navy cardigan or blazer with school crest, white knee socks or navy tights, and dark dress shoes (e.g., navy/black Mary Janes or saddle shoes).
* **Middle School:** White tailored oxford shirt, school plaid pleated skirt, navy uniform blazer with Duchesne crest, navy or white socks, dark dress flats.
* **Upper School:** White oxford button-down shirt, senior/upper-school plaid pleated skirt, navy blazer with crest, appropriate dress flats.

### B. Regular Daily Uniform
* Navy or white pique knit polo shirt with embroidered Duchesne crest.
* Division plaid skirt, skort, or tailored navy uniform shorts/pants.
* White or navy crew socks; athletic sneakers or approved uniform shoes.

### C. Friday Spirit Wear Days
* Duchesne spirit t-shirts, athletic team shirts, or Charger class shirts.
* Duchesne outerwear, hoodies, and sweatshirts purchased from the Duchesne Spirit Store.
* Uniform bottoms or approved blue denim jeans (on designated Spirit Jeans days).

### D. Accessories & Hair
* Duchesne plaid hair bows, ribbons, and headbands available at the Spirit Store.
* Simple jewelry adhering to division guidelines.

## 7. Security, Privacy & Zero-Password Architecture

The Duchesne School Assistant operates strictly under an uncompromising **Zero-Password Policy**:

* **No Credential Harvesting:** The assistant **NEVER** asks for, stores, or processes parents' Veracross, Toddle, or Magnus passwords.
* **No Unauthorized Scraping:** The assistant does not bypass school login protections or access confidential student educational records.
* **Privacy By Design:**
  * **Public Information:** Public school calendars, dining menus, social posts, and spirit store items are aggregated via public feeds.
  * **Private Information:** For personalized student notes or private teacher emails, parents copy-and-paste specific text into their own AI sessions for synthesis, or run local agent workflows on their own computer where cookies stay securely within their local browser profile.
* **Student Privacy Compliance:** Adheres to family and student privacy standards by ensuring student records and personal directory information remain strictly client-side.
