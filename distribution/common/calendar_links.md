# Duchesne Academy Calendar Subscriptions (1-Click Setup)

Subscribe directly to **Duchesne Academy of the Sacred Heart** calendars across your phone, tablet, and computer. Once subscribed, school holidays, liturgies, and campus events sync automatically to your personal schedule.

---

## 1-Click All-School Calendar Subscriptions

### 🍏 Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad & Mac)
Tap or click the link below to subscribe in your device's native Calendar app:
* [**Subscribe in Apple Calendar (1-Click)**](webcal://portals.veracross.com/duchesne/subscribe/all_school.ics)
* *Protocol:* Native `webcal://` feed automatically prompts iOS and macOS to add and sync the calendar.

### 📅 Google Calendar (Android & Web)
Click the link below to add the Duchesne calendar directly to your Google account:
* [**Subscribe in Google Calendar (1-Click)**](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r?cid=https%3A%2F%2Fportals.veracross.com%2Fduchesne%2Fsubscribe%2Fall_school.ics)
* *Manual fallback:* Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) > Click **+** next to *Other calendars* > Select **From URL** > Paste:  
  `https://portals.veracross.com/duchesne/subscribe/all_school.ics`

### ✉️ Microsoft Outlook (Web & Desktop)
Click the link below to open Outlook's calendar subscription interface:
* [**Add to Microsoft Outlook**](https://outlook.office.com/calendar/addcalendar)
* *Steps:*
  1. Click the link above (or open Outlook Calendar and click **Add calendar**).
  2. Select **Subscribe from web** on the left menu.
  3. Paste the calendar feed URL:  
     `https://portals.veracross.com/duchesne/subscribe/all_school.ics`
  4. Name it **Duchesne Academy All-School** and click **Import**.

## Personalized Family & Student Calendars

Veracross provides private, personalized feeds including each student's specific class schedule, division events, and athletics:

1. Log in to the [Veracross Parent Portal](https://portals.veracross.com/duchesne/parent).
2. Navigate to **Calendar** (or go to `https://portals.veracross.com/duchesne/parent/calendar`).
3. Click the **Subscribe** button in the upper right.
4. Copy your personalized feed link (format: `https://portals.veracross.com/duchesne/subscribe/<your-personal-token>.ics`).
5. Subscribe using your device's preferred method:
   * **Apple Calendar:** Replace `https://` with `webcal://` (e.g. `webcal://portals.veracross.com/duchesne/subscribe/<your-personal-token>.ics`) and tap to open.
   * **Google Calendar:** Use the format `https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r?cid=https%3A%2F%2Fportals.veracross.com%2Fduchesne%2Fsubscribe%2F<your-personal-token>.ics`.
   * **Outlook:** In Outlook, click **Add calendar > Subscribe from web** and paste your personalized feed link.
